Roasted Garlic & Rosemary Focaccia

⏱ 2h 15m total 🍽 8 servings ◆ Medium

Ingredients

8 servings
  • 500g bread flour
  • 375ml warm water
  • 7g instant yeast
  • 10g fine sea salt
  • 6 tbsp olive oil, divided
  • 1 whole head garlic, roasted
  • 2 tbsp fresh rosemary, chopped
  • Flaky salt, to finish

Method

  1. Mix flour, yeast, and salt in a large bowl. Add warm water and 2 tbsp olive oil, stir until a shaggy dough forms.
  2. Cover and let rest 10 minutes, then stretch-and-fold the dough 4 times. Repeat every 30 minutes for 2 hours.
  3. Pour 2 tbsp olive oil into a baking pan. Transfer the dough in and gently stretch to fill the pan.
  4. Cover and proof until doubled and full of bubbles, about 45 minutes.
  5. Dimple the dough all over with oiled fingers. Press in roasted garlic cloves and scatter rosemary.
  6. Drizzle with remaining olive oil and a generous pinch of flaky salt.
  7. Bake at 220°C (425°F) for 22–25 minutes, until deeply golden.
  8. Cool for at least 15 minutes before slicing — it's worth the wait.
